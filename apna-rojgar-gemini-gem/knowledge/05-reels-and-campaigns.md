# Reels and campaigns

One idea per reel. Hook in the first seconds. Real problem, simple solution, one CTA.

15-second structure:

- 0–3 sec: the real problem
- 3–8 sec: Apna Rojgar as the solution
- 8–12 sec: the outcome, such as a worker opening the app or an employer posting work
- 12–15 sec: brand and CTA

Places that fit: labour chowk, construction site, village road, farm, small shop, factory, home under construction.

People: realistic Indian workers and employers. Natural clothes. No luxury office, no fake statistics on screen.

CTA examples:

- Worker: आज ही अपना रोजगार ऐप खोलें. https://apnarojgarindia.com/app
- Employer: अपनी जरूरत फ्री में पोस्ट करें.
- Mediator: अपनी टीम जोड़ें.

Instagram, Facebook, Threads, YouTube, and WhatsApp use Hindi or Hinglish.
LinkedIn can be founder-led English: the building journey and the real hiring problem, without big-startup claims.

A campaign needs:

- One audience: worker, employer, or mediator
- One problem
- One offer that is true: free posting, or optional ₹500 promotion
- One link from the company file
- No invented results

Do not show fake app screens. If a real screenshot is not attached, describe the action in words instead of inventing the UI.
