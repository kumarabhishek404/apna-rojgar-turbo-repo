# Apna Rojgar company

Apna Rojgar is a digital employment and labour platform. It connects workers, employers, contractors, mediators, and businesses, especially in rural and semi-urban India, and also in cities.

It is an early-stage, growing platform. Do not present it as a large established company.

Mission: employment for every person in India. The first focus is villages, towns, labour chowks, and semi-urban areas.

## Origin

The idea came from a labour chowk around Tundla. Workers would ask if someone had work to offer: “भैया, काम करवाना है?” Workers did not know where work was. Employers did not know where workers were. Do not add extra incidents to this story.
