# Apna Rojgar brand

Product name: Apna Rojgar.

Do not rename it to ApnaRojgar, Apna Rozgar, or Apna Rojgar India.

Meaning: a platform that belongs to everyone looking for work or workers.

Personality: human, helpful, trustworthy, Indian, simple, practical, local, rural-friendly, modern without looking like a luxury startup.

## Marketing colors

Use these on posters, reels, and ads.

- Dark blue #22409A is the main color.
- White #FFFFFF is the background and the text on blue.
- Orange #FF9933 is one small accent only.

## In-app colors

Use these only when describing or mocking the real app. Do not recolor the app with #FF9933.

- Primary blue #22409a
- Text #2C2C2C
- Screen background #F5F5F5
- Soft blue panels #EAF0FF
- In-app orange #FF7A00 and #fa6400
- Success #4CAF50
- Error #DC3545
- Unselected tab #5F7BA8
- Selected tab: white icon on #22409a

## Type

- Posters: large, simple Hindi or Hinglish. No decorative or luxury fonts.
- Website body: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif.
- Mobile app: the phone’s system font. Hindi text is set slightly larger than English. Do not claim the app uses Poppins, Inter, or Noto.

## Logo

The Gem knowledge file `logo.png` is the official logo.

Blue circle. White construction worker: hard hat, simple head and shoulders, high-visibility vest. A white band carries blue Hindi text: अपना रोज़गार.

Use that uploaded file. Do not redesign, recolor, crop the wordmark, or ask an image model to invent a new logo. If the image tool cannot place the file, leave empty space and say the official logo must be added from `logo.png`.

Do not use any mark that says KAARYA or “कुशल बनो, सफल रहो”. That is not the Apna Rojgar logo.

## Pictures

Prefer realistic Indian people, labour chowks, construction sites, village roads, farms, small shops, factories, and homes being built. Avoid luxury offices, neon, crowded layouts, tiny text, Western stock scenes, and exaggerated poverty.
