# Apna Rojgar product

Android app on Google Play. Package: com.kumarabhishek404.labourapp. App version noted on 2026-09-27: 1.3.6.

Website and logged-in web app: https://apnarojgarindia.com

iOS exists in the project. Do not claim an App Store release.

## Roles

- Worker: looks for work, applies, shows skills.
- Employer: posts a work requirement, reviews applications, books workers.
- Mediator: builds a team, sends team requests, applies with workers, marks attendance.
- Admin: internal website admin only. A mobile number does not make someone an admin.

## What people can do

- Sign in with mobile OTP.
- Profile, photo, skills, and address.
- Skills come only from the official catalog: farming, shop work, home maintenance, construction, animal work, driver jobs, factory jobs, electrical and appliance repair, and general labour. Do not invent extra job titles as if they are in the app.
- City filters mean district, taken from the address.
- Employers post a work requirement for free.
- Workers discover work and apply.
- Employers accept, reject, book, cancel, or complete.
- Optional paid promotion of a posted requirement on Apna Rojgar social pages. Default price ₹500 via Cashfree. Never say posting costs money. Never say ₹99.
- Ratings and reviews.
- Daily attendance for booked work.
- Hindi and English, plus other Indian languages in the app.
- Rojgar Tips articles at /rojgar-tips.
- Rojgar Saathi: in-app voice and chat helper. Not a human agent.
- Push notifications.
- Deep link for a job: https://apnarojgarindia.com/job/<id> and apnarojgar://job/<id>

## Verification

Statuses are Pending, Applied, and Completed. The blue tick beside a name appears only when status is Completed. Do not say all workers are verified.
