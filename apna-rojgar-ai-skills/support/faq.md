# APNA ROJGAR — FAQ

Answer only from confirmed knowledge. If a fact is missing, say `[NEEDS CONFIRMATION]`.

## What is Apna Rojgar?

Apna Rojgar is a digital employment and labour platform that connects people who need work with people who need workers.

## Who is it for?

Workers, employers, and mediators or contractors, especially in rural and semi-urban India, and also urban users.

## Where can I download the app?

Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kumarabhishek404.labourapp

## What is the official website?

https://apnarojgarindia.com

## What does it cost to post a requirement?

Posting a work requirement is free.

Promoting that requirement on Apna Rojgar’s social pages is a separate paid option. The current default is ₹500. Do not quote ₹99.

## How do I contact support?

Email info@apnarojgarindia.com or call +91 6397308499. The WhatsApp community link is in `company/company-overview.md`.

## Are all workers verified?

No. A verified tick appears only when that person’s verification status is Completed. Others are Pending or Applied.

## Is Apna Rojgar a large established company?

No. It is an early-stage and growing platform.
