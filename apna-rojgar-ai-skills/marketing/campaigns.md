# APNA ROJGAR — CAMPAIGNS

Campaigns should follow the messaging in `marketing/messaging.md`.

Structure:

1. Problem
2. Person experiencing the problem
3. Apna Rojgar solution
4. Simple action or CTA

Do not invent statistics, partnerships, or unsupported ranking claims.
