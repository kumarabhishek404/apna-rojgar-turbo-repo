# APNA ROJGAR — CONTENT EXAMPLES

## App and UX copy

App copy should be:

- Short
- Clear
- Action-oriented
- Easy for rural and semi-urban users
- Hindi and Hinglish friendly

Preferred examples:

- काम खोजें
- काम पोस्ट करें
- कामगार खोजें
- Apply करें
- मेरी सेवाएँ
- Booked Workers
- मेरी टीम
- Attendance
- काम की जानकारी
- आवेदन
- बात करें

Avoid long explanatory paragraphs inside buttons or small UI elements.

Prefer clarity over sophisticated English.

Check whether the screen is for a worker, employer, or mediator/contractor before writing the line.

## Spoken and caption lines

- “काम चाहिए?”
- “कामगार चाहिए?”
- “अपनी जरूरत पोस्ट करें.”
- “अपने आसपास काम के मौके देखें.”

Caption shape: Hook → Context → Solution → CTA
