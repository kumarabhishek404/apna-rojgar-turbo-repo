# APNA ROJGAR — REELS

## Rules

- Hook in the first seconds
- One idea per video
- Real problem
- Simple solution
- Clear CTA
- Realistic Indian people and environments
- Visuals that are natural and easy to understand

Use a real app screenshot or the official logo when the asset is available. Do not ask an AI tool to recreate them.

## 15-second structure

Duration: 15 seconds

- 0–3 sec: show the real problem
- 3–8 sec: introduce Apna Rojgar as the solution
- 8–12 sec: show the relevant app interaction or outcome
- 12–15 sec: show the brand or logo and the CTA
