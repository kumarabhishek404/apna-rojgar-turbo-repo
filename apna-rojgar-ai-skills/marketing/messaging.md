# APNA ROJGAR — MESSAGING

## Objective

Communicate a practical solution to real employment and worker-hiring problems.

Do not make every post a generic “download our app” advertisement.

Structure:

1. Problem
2. Person experiencing the problem
3. Apna Rojgar solution
4. Simple action or CTA

## Worker message

Problem: काम ढूँढना मुश्किल हो सकता है.

Solution: Apna Rojgar पर काम के मौके खोजें और अपनी services दिखाएँ.

## Employer message

Problem: सही कामगार ढूँढना मुश्किल हो सकता है.

Solution: Apna Rojgar पर अपनी जरूरत पोस्ट करें और कामगार खोजें.

## Contractor / mediator message

Problem: टीम और काम के अवसर को coordinate करना.

Solution: Apna Rojgar के worker/team features का उपयोग करें.

## Facts to keep in every message

- Posting work is free. Paid promotion is optional and currently defaults to ₹500.
- Do not say every worker is verified.
- Use the links in `company/company-overview.md` only.

## Core direction

“काम भी, कामगार भी”

Other possible messages:

- काम ढूँढना आसान
- कामगार ढूँढना आसान
- काम और कामगारों का अपना प्लेटफॉर्म
- रोजगार के मौके

Do not use all messages together. Choose the one that fits the campaign.

## Style

Should be:

- Simple
- Human
- Relatable
- Direct
- Useful
- Trustworthy

Avoid:

- Fake urgency
- Fake scarcity
- Fake statistics
- Unsupported “#1” claims
- “India's largest” without evidence
- Exaggerated success

## Approved terms

Prefer:

- Apna Rojgar
- मजदूर
- कामगार
- काम देने वाला
- काम करने वाला
- ठेकेदार
- काम
- रोजगार
- काम की जरूरत
- काम का मौका
- कामगार की जरूरत
- काम पोस्ट करें
- काम खोजें
- कामगार खोजें

Use carefully:

- Blue-collar jobs — useful in professional or business contexts, but explain in simple language for consumer audiences.

Avoid as default:

- Labourers — use “मजदूर” when speaking directly to Indian consumer audiences
- Workforce ecosystem
- Talent acquisition
- Human capital
- Employment orchestration
- Labour optimization

Those phrases can be used only when a professional document genuinely requires them.
