# APNA ROJGAR — BRAND GUIDELINES

## Brand name

Primary brand name: **Apna Rojgar**

Use “Apna Rojgar” as the default name.

Avoid unnecessarily changing it to:

- ApnaRojgar
- Apna Rojgar India
- ApnaRojgarIndia
- Apna Rozgar

## Brand meaning

“Apna Rojgar” means a platform that belongs to everyone looking for work or workers.

## Brand personality

Apna Rojgar should feel:

- Human
- Helpful
- Trustworthy
- Indian
- Grounded
- Simple
- Accessible
- Modern
- Practical
- Hopeful
- Community-oriented
- Local
- Inclusive
- Rural-friendly

Avoid:

- Overly corporate language
- Luxury branding
- Complicated jargon
- Artificial or futuristic presentation
- Exaggerated claims
- Fake success imagery

The communication should feel like a helpful person explaining something to a worker, employer, contractor, or mediator.

## Core brand thought

Technology should feel like it is solving a real employment problem for real people.
