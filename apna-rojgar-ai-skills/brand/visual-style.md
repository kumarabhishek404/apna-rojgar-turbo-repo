# APNA ROJGAR — VISUAL STYLE

## Direction

Preferred:

- Clean
- Modern
- Mobile-first
- High readability
- Realistic Indian environments
- Rural and semi-urban context when relevant
- Large readable text
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Simple CTA

For rural audiences, readability is more important than decorative design.

## Composition

Prefer:

- One clear message
- One primary visual
- One clear CTA
- Strong spacing
- High contrast
- Phone-screen readability

Avoid:

- Crowded layouts
- Tiny text
- Excessive gradients
- Too many colors
- Decorative elements that compete with the message

## People and photography

When people are shown:

- Prefer realistic Indian people
- Use culturally appropriate clothing and environments
- Show authentic work situations
- Avoid stereotypical or exaggerated poverty imagery
- Avoid generic Western stock-photo environments for Indian employment stories

## Rural and semi-urban settings

Useful environments:

- Labour chowk
- Construction site
- Village road
- Farm
- Small shop
- Factory
- Local market
- Residential construction
- Worker gathering

## Type and UI

Follow `brand/typography.md` and `brand/colors.md`.

For posters: blue, white, and one orange accent. Large Hindi or Hinglish. One message.

For app mockups: system font, blue `#22409a`, light gray background `#F5F5F5`, white cards, and the in-app oranges `#FF7A00` / `#fa6400`. Do not redraw the app in a new font.

Buttons are rounded. Role and status use small pills. The verified mark is a small blue tick beside the name, only for completed verification.
