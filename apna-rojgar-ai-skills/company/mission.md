# APNA ROJGAR — MISSION

Make employment opportunities and workers easier to find for people across India.

Broader mission:

> Employment for every person in India.

Apna Rojgar is not positioned only as a conventional startup or business. It is also a movement focused on making employment opportunities easier to discover and making workers easier to find.

The initial focus is on solving the real-world employment problems faced by people in villages, towns, labour chowks, and semi-urban areas.

Long-term vision: build a movement for employment for every person in India.
