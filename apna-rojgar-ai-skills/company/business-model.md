# APNA ROJGAR — BUSINESS MODEL

## Current stage

Apna Rojgar is an early-stage and growing platform.

Do not present it as a huge established company.

## Marketplace

Supply: workers and service providers

Demand: employers, contractors, and businesses

## Monetization direction

Current product direction includes:

- Free requirement posting
- Paid social promotion of a requirement

The current default promotion price is ₹500. Details and the rule against older prices are in `product/pricing.md`.

Do not invent pricing. Pricing rules also live in `product/pricing.md`.

## Strategic considerations

When giving business advice, consider:

- Worker acquisition
- Employer acquisition
- Local density
- Trust
- Verification
- Matching
- Retention
- Reviews
- Ratings
- Community
- Fraud prevention
- Monetization
- Network effects

Never use fabricated metrics to justify a strategy.
