# APNA ROJGAR — COMPANY OVERVIEW

## What is Apna Rojgar?

Apna Rojgar is a digital employment and labour platform connecting workers, employers, contractors, mediators, and businesses.

It is designed to make finding work and finding workers easier, especially across rural and semi-urban India.

## Core sectors

- Construction
- Manufacturing
- Delivery
- Services
- Agriculture and farm work
- Local businesses
- Maintenance
- Factories
- Household services
- Other blue-collar work

## Positioning

Apna Rojgar is not only a conventional job portal. It is focused on practical local employment and worker discovery.

It is an early-stage and growing platform. Do not present it as a huge established company.

## Origin story

The idea came from seeing the real problem at a labour chowk around Tundla.

Workers would ask:

“भैया, काम करवाना है?”

That experience highlighted the difficulty workers face when searching for work and the difficulty employers face when searching for reliable workers.

Apna Rojgar was created to bring this offline problem into a simpler digital platform.
